# Astrology foundations: free course outline

## Start with the birth chart

Learn what a chart records, how planets, signs, houses, and aspects fit together, and what birth data can confirm.

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [A Chart Is a Symbolic Map](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/a-chart-is-a-symbolic-map/) | Understand a natal chart as calculated sky positions for a specific time and place, and distinguish those facts from astrological interpretation. | 9 |
| 2 | [Zodiac Signs and Chart Coordinates](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/zodiac-signs-and-chart-coordinates/) | Understand the twelve equal signs of the tropical zodiac and avoid confusing astrological signs with the uneven boundaries of astronomical constellations. | 9 |
| 3 | [Four Layers of a Chart](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/four-layers-of-a-chart/) | Identify planets, signs, houses, and aspects so each interpretation can be traced to chart evidence. | 9 |
| 4 | [Birth Data and What Remains Unknown](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/birth-data-and-what-remains-unknown/) | Match the scope of a reading to the reliability of birth data. If the time is unknown, do not invent a precise Moon degree, Ascendant, or house placements. | 9 |

## Planets and the three core points

Explore the Sun, Moon, Ascendant, personal planets, and slower planets without treating a chart position as a fixed personality label.

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | [Sun and Moon: Expression and Needs](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/sun-and-moon-expression-and-needs/) | Compare the Sun’s and Moon’s introductory themes without treating either placement as a complete personality description. | 9 |
| 6 | [Ascendant: Chart Entry and Limits](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/ascendant-chart-entry-and-limits/) | Distinguish the Ascendant’s exact degree from the start of the first whole-sign house, and understand that the MC is a separate point that need not be in the tenth house. | 9 |
| 7 | [Mercury, Venus, and Mars](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/mercury-venus-and-mars/) | Practice using three introductory entry points: thinking and communication, value and connection, and action and assertion. | 9 |
| 8 | [Jupiter to Pluto: Scope and Generational Context](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/jupiter-to-pluto-scope-and-generational-context/) | Recognize introductory Jupiter and Saturn themes and the longer timescale of outer planets without turning shared generational placements into personal predictions. | 9 |

## The expressive language of the twelve signs

Learn the sign order, four elements, and three modalities, then use them to describe a planet’s style rather than label a person.

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | [Elements: Four Ways to Frame a Theme](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/elements-four-ways-to-frame-a-theme/) | Use fire, earth, air, and water as a memory aid for symbolic themes, not as personality categories. | 9 |
| 10 | [Three Modalities: Initiate, Sustain, Adapt](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/three-modalities-initiate-sustain-adapt/) | Use cardinal, fixed, and mutable signs to describe different rhythms of action without ranking them. | 9 |
| 11 | [The Twelve Signs at a Glance](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/the-twelve-signs-at-a-glance/) | Learn the order, elements, and modalities of the twelve signs so you can look them up rather than memorize personality claims. | 9 |
| 12 | [Signs Modify Planet Themes](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/signs-modify-planet-themes/) | Combine a planet’s theme with a sign’s style to form a complete but revisable symbolic sentence. | 9 |

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | [Houses 1–4: Self, Resources, Daily Life, Roots](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/houses-1-4-self-resources-daily-life-roots/) | Use the first four houses as broad areas for questions, not as promises of events. | 9 |
| 14 | [Houses 5–8: Expression, Work, and Relationships](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/houses-5-8-expression-work-and-relationships/) | Use houses 5–8 to name areas for exploration; do not turn them into a fixed life script. | 9 |
| 15 | [Houses 9–12: Learning, Public Life, and Retreat](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/houses-9-12-learning-public-life-and-retreat/) | Relate the final four houses to wider horizons, public roles, community, and private space without using fear-based claims. | 9 |
| 16 | [Whole-Sign Practice and Unknown Birth Times](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/whole-sign-practice-and-unknown-birth-times/) | Map whole-sign houses only from a valid Ascendant. Respect calculation warnings and never silently switch house systems. | 9 |

## Major aspects and interaction

Calculate the five major aspects and their orbs, then explore how two planetary themes may relate.

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17 | [How Aspects Are Calculated](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/how-aspects-are-calculated/) | An aspect is based on the angular distance between two ecliptic longitudes. Use the chart’s calculated result rather than guessing from elements or keywords. | 9 |
| 18 | [Conjunction, Sextile, and Trine](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/conjunction-sextile-and-trine/) | Compare common language for focus, opportunity, and ease while noticing both resources and possible inertia. | 9 |
| 19 | [Square and Opposition: Describe Tension Clearly](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/square-and-opposition-describe-tension-clearly/) | Read challenging aspects as a conversation between needs. Avoid fear, fatalism, and promises of harm. | 9 |
| 20 | [Combine Aspects with Signs and Houses](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/combine-aspects-with-signs-and-houses/) | Place an aspect in the context of planetary functions and house topics to create a concise, traceable reading. | 9 |

## Read the chart, not a label

Clarify the question, choose a few reliable placements, and combine them into an open interpretation that can be checked against experience.

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 21 | [Clarify What the User Wants to Ask](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/clarify-what-the-user-wants-to-ask/) | Turn a broad request into one present-day topic that astrology can frame for reflection, while keeping the user in control. | 9 |
| 22 | [Choose Three Useful Clues](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/choose-three-useful-clues/) | Use the sequence “question → chart evidence → tentative theme” to avoid listing irrelevant placements. | 9 |
| 23 | [Turn Placements into a Testable Theme](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/turn-placements-into-a-testable-theme/) | Organize keywords into possible resources, friction, real-life examples, and a question that could challenge your reading. | 9 |
| 24 | [Show Uncertainty and Revise](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/show-uncertainty-and-revise/) | Set honest limits around an interpretation and update it when the user offers a different experience. | 9 |

## Observe and review transits

Separate natal and transit snapshots, keep dated observations, and use counterexamples to refine—not prove—an interpretation.

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | [Natal Charts and Transits Are Different Data](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/natal-charts-and-transits-are-different-data/) | Keep the natal birth moment, the transit reference time, and the record’s creation time distinct. Each refers to a different snapshot. | 9 |
| 26 | [Read Transits as Themes, Not Promises](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/read-transits-as-themes-not-promises/) | Use transits to suggest topics for observation and user-chosen preparation, not guaranteed events. | 9 |
| 27 | [Keep an Open-Ended Astrology Journal](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/keep-an-open-ended-astrology-journal/) | Record lived experience, alternative explanations, and days that do not fit to reduce selective recall. | 9 |
| 28 | [Review Without Turning It into Identity](https://astrea.secondwindltd.xyz/learn/review-without-turning-it-into-identity/) | Avoid turning a few experiences into a fixed personality claim. Revise interpretations in light of the limits of the record. | 9 |
