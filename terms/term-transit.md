# What does Transit mean in astrology?

The sky positions at a selected time and their calculated relationships to natal positions.

## How do you read it?

Keep the snapshot time, transiting planet, natal point, and calculated aspect explicit; do not invent events.

## Example

A transit Mercury aspect to a practice chart’s natal Mars can be used as an observation prompt for that date.

## A question to ask yourself

> Which time snapshot does this reading use, and which natal point does it connect to?

## What is the common misreading?

A transit cannot establish that a specific event will happen, and it does not replace natal data.
