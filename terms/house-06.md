# What does 6th House mean in astrology?

Daily routines, skill practice, work tasks, and care

## How do you read it?

Use this house placement only when the Ascendant and selected house system are reliable. Pair the house theme with any planet placed there and with the user's question.

## Example

Mars in the 6th house can prompt questions about task pace and energy use; it is not a basis for diagnosing illness.

## A question to ask yourself

> Which everyday step would be most useful to reorganize?

## What is the common misreading?

A house describes a symbolic life area, not proof that an event will happen. Without a reliable birth time, do not show the Ascendant, house signs, or house placements.
