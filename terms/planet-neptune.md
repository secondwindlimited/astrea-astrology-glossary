# What does Neptune mean in astrology?

In modern astrology, a symbol for imagination, ideals, empathy, and sometimes blurred boundaries.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

A calculated Neptune–Mercury aspect can prompt questions about how imagination and fact-checking work together.

## A question to ask yourself

> What sparks your imagination, and how do you check the facts?

## What is the common misreading?

Neptune cannot diagnose addiction, deception, or a mental health condition.
