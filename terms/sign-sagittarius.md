# What does Sagittarius mean in astrology?

Fire sign · Mutable. Often used to explore exploration, learning, and seeking meaning.

## How do you read it?

Read this sign as a style for a planet's expression, alongside the planet's theme, its house, any calculated aspects, and the person's question. Elements and modalities describe symbolic patterns, not fixed personality traits.

## Example

Jupiter in Sagittarius can prompt a question about what someone wants to learn or explore next.

## A question to ask yourself

> What would you like to learn or explore next?

## What is the common misreading?

This is a symbolic association, not a fixed personality trait or a prediction.
