# What does Mercury mean in astrology?

Often used to explore thinking, learning, information processing, and communication.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

Mercury in Virgo can prompt a question about sorting details and organizing information.

## A question to ask yourself

> How do you usually make complex information easier to understand?

## What is the common misreading?

A Mercury sign does not determine intelligence or prove that someone is good at every kind of communication.
