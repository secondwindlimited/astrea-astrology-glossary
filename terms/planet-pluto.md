# What does Pluto mean in astrology?

In modern astrology, used to explore themes such as power, deep change, control, and renewal.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

A Pluto sign change can be treated as shared historical context, not an individual forecast.

## A question to ask yourself

> What long-used form of power or control are you reconsidering?

## What is the common misreading?

Pluto’s position cannot predict death, trauma, disaster, or an irreversible fate.
