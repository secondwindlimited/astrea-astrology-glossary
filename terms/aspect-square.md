# What does Square (90°) mean in astrology?

Two planets are about 90° apart, within the stated orb. Symbolically, a square can frame friction between needs that may call for adjustment.

## How do you read it?

Confirm the measured angular distance and the chart's stated orb before naming an aspect. Then read both planet themes with their signs, houses, and the person's context.

## Example

A Mercury–Saturn square can prompt reflection on how the urge to speak and careful review affect each other.

## A question to ask yourself

> How could care and preparation support your expression instead of stopping it?

## What is the common misreading?

A square is not a prediction of bad events. Explain both planet themes and check the interpretation against the person’s actual situation.
