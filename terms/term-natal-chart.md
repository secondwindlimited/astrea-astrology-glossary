# What does Natal Chart mean in astrology?

A chart calculated from a person’s birth time and place, used as the data basis for natal readings.

## How do you read it?

Record input precision, tropical zodiac, and house system; a missing birth time limits which chart layers can be shown.

## Example

A natal Moon in Gemini in the 10th house is a birth-chart placement, not the Moon’s position today.

## A question to ask yourself

> Is this placement from the natal chart or from the sky on a selected date?

## What is the common misreading?

A natal chart is not a scientifically validated measure of personality or fate, and daily transits do not change its birth data.
