# What does Venus mean in astrology?

A symbolic prompt for values, connection, appreciation, and reciprocity.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

Venus in Libra can open questions about fairness, negotiation, and shared tastes.

## A question to ask yourself

> What helps you feel respected in a collaboration?

## What is the common misreading?

Venus cannot guarantee romance, marriage, or financial results.
