# What does Tropical Zodiac mean in astrology?

An astrological coordinate system anchored to the equinoxes, divided into twelve 30° sign sectors.

## How do you read it?

Name the coordinate system being used and keep astrological signs distinct from astronomical constellations.

## Example

If Astrea shows the Sun in Libra, it means its tropical zodiac longitude falls in the Libra sector.

## A question to ask yourself

> Are we discussing an astrological sign coordinate or an astronomical constellation boundary?

## What is the common misreading?

The shared names do not mean astrological signs and astronomical constellations use the same boundaries.
