# What does Opposition (180°) mean in astrology?

Two planets are about 180° apart, within the stated orb. Symbolically, an opposition can highlight two needs that may require coordination.

## How do you read it?

Confirm the measured angular distance and the chart's stated orb before naming an aspect. Then read both planet themes with their signs, houses, and the person's context.

## Example

A Venus–Mars opposition can open a discussion about balancing cooperation with personal initiative.

## A question to ask yourself

> How can you respect both cooperation and your own boundaries?

## What is the common misreading?

An opposition does not guarantee conflict, separation, or hostility. Describe both sides and ask the person how they experience them.
