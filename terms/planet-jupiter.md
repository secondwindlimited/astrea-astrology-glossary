# What does Jupiter mean in astrology?

A symbolic clue for expansion, learning, beliefs, and looking for opportunities.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

Jupiter in the 9th house can open questions about study, travel, or a wider worldview.

## A question to ask yourself

> Where would you like to broaden your experience?

## What is the common misreading?

Jupiter is not a guarantee of good luck and does not remove real-world risks.
