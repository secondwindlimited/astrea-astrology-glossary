# What does Natal and Transit Positions mean in astrology?

A natal position belongs to the birth chart; a transit position belongs to the sky at a selected time. A calculation connects them.

## How do you read it?

Confirm the identity and time of each position before discussing how a transit relates to a natal theme.

## Example

“Natal Venus in Libra” is a birth-chart placement; “Venus in Aquarius this week” describes a transit. They are not interchangeable.

## A question to ask yourself

> Which time frame does each of these two planetary positions belong to?

## What is the common misreading?

Do not turn a transit into a natal placement or treat today’s sky as a permanent personality description.
