# What does Birth-Data Precision mean in astrology?

The source and reliability of birth date, place, and time; precision determines which chart details can be used.

## How do you read it?

If the birth time is missing, keep confirmed stable placements but do not infer the Ascendant, houses, or house placements.

## Example

If only the birth date is known, the app leaves the Ascendant and houses unknown.

## A question to ask yourself

> How reliable is each placement, and which conclusions must be left out?

## What is the common misreading?

Approximate input must not be presented as a precise degree-level fact.
