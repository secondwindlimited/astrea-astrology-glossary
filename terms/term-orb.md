# What does Orb mean in astrology?

The difference between the measured angular distance and the exact angle of an aspect; a smaller orb is closer to exact.

## How do you read it?

Calculate the difference from the aspect’s exact angle, then apply the chart’s stated orb convention.

## Example

At 92°, two planets are 2° from an exact 90° square, so the orb is 2°.

## A question to ask yourself

> How many degrees separate this aspect from its exact angle?

## What is the common misreading?

An orb is a measurement convention; it does not predict how intensely someone will experience an event.
