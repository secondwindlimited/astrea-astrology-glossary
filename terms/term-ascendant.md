# What does Ascendant mean in astrology?

The ecliptic point rising on the eastern horizon for a given birth time and place; in this course it anchors the 1st whole-sign house.

## How do you read it?

Verify the birth data first, then use the Ascendant sign to map the twelve whole-sign houses.

## Example

When a practice chart confirms Virgo rising, the whole sign of Virgo is the 1st house.

## A question to ask yourself

> How reliable is the birth time? If it is unknown, how should the Ascendant be shown?

## What is the common misreading?

The Ascendant is not the Sun sign and cannot be reliably guessed from someone’s appearance or personality.
