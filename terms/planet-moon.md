# What does Moon mean in astrology?

A symbolic prompt for emotional needs, familiar responses, and ways of giving or receiving care.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

The Moon in Gemini can prompt questions about using conversation, variety, or organizing information to understand feelings.

## A question to ask yourself

> What kind of daily rhythm or communication helps you feel more at ease?

## What is the common misreading?

A Moon placement cannot diagnose mental health or predict an emotional event.
