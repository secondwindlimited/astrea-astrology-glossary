# What does Mars mean in astrology?

Often used to explore motivation to act, initiative, conflict responses, and boundaries.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

Mars in Aries can prompt a question about starting quickly or trying something directly.

## A question to ask yourself

> When you meet resistance, how do you usually begin to act?

## What is the common misreading?

Mars does not mean violence and cannot by itself establish that a particular event will occur.
