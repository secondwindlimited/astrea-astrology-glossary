# What does Whole-Sign Houses mean in astrology?

A house system in which the Ascendant sign as a whole is the 1st house, followed by signs in zodiacal order.

## How do you read it?

Confirm a reliable Ascendant before mapping house placements; Astrea uses this system for its course examples.

## Example

With Gemini rising, Gemini is the 1st house, Cancer the 2nd, and Leo the 3rd.

## A question to ask yourself

> Do you know which house system this chart uses?

## What is the common misreading?

Whole-sign houses are not the only astrological method; house boundaries differ across systems.
