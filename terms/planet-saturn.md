# What does Saturn mean in astrology?

Often used to discuss responsibility, limits, structure, patience, and long-term practice.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

Saturn in the 6th house can prompt a conversation about daily routines, work responsibilities, and practicing skills.

## A question to ask yourself

> What kind of structure supports you without overwhelming you?

## What is the common misreading?

Saturn does not mean punishment, failure, or success that is inevitably delayed.
