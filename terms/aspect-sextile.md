# What does Sextile (60°) mean in astrology?

Two planets are about 60° apart, within the chart’s stated orb. In symbolic reading, a sextile can point to a resource that may be developed through participation.

## How do you read it?

Confirm the measured angular distance and the chart's stated orb before naming an aspect. Then read both planet themes with their signs, houses, and the person's context.

## Example

A Venus–Mars sextile can open a question about how a wish to connect supports action and expression.

## A question to ask yourself

> What action could help you make use of this possible resource?

## What is the common misreading?

A sextile does not guarantee that an opportunity will appear automatically, and it cannot be inferred from sign elements alone.
