# What does Sun mean in astrology?

A symbolic prompt for self-expression, the direction of effort, and a sense of vitality.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

The Sun in Taurus can open questions about steadiness, sustained effort, and sensory experience.

## A question to ask yourself

> Where are you most willing to keep investing your effort?

## What is the common misreading?

The Sun is not an objective measure of a person’s whole personality, career, or life purpose.
