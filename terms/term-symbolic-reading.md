# What does Symbolic Reading mean in astrology?

An interpretation that organizes chart symbols into a discussion topic within a tradition, then lets personal experience confirm or revise it.

## How do you read it?

Separate calculated facts, traditional meanings, personal hypotheses, and lived experience.

## Example

“Venus in the 2nd house can open a question about values” is a symbolic prompt; “you will become wealthy” is a prediction.

## A question to ask yourself

> Which part of this sentence is calculated fact, and which part is your interpretive hypothesis?

## What is the common misreading?

A symbolic system is not a scientific forecast, medical diagnosis, or authority over someone’s decisions.
