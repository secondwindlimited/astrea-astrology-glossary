# What does Conjunction (0°) mean in astrology?

Two planets share the same ecliptic longitude within the stated orb. In symbolic reading, their themes may feel joined or concentrated.

## How do you read it?

Confirm the measured angular distance and the chart's stated orb before naming an aspect. Then read both planet themes with their signs, houses, and the person's context.

## Example

Mercury conjunct Jupiter can prompt a question about how communication and a desire to expand knowledge work together.

## A question to ask yourself

> When do these two themes show up together in your life?

## What is the common misreading?

A conjunction can bring two themes together, but it does not guarantee harmony or good luck.
