# What does Trine (120°) mean in astrology?

Two planets are about 120° apart, within the stated orb. Symbolically, a trine may describe an easier flow or an available resource.

## How do you read it?

Confirm the measured angular distance and the chart's stated orb before naming an aspect. Then read both planet themes with their signs, houses, and the person's context.

## Example

A Moon–Venus trine can prompt questions about how care needs and ways of connecting may support each other.

## A question to ask yourself

> Can you recognize this ease as a resource without taking it for granted?

## What is the common misreading?

A trine does not guarantee success; an easy flow can also become habitual or go unnoticed.
