# What does Uranus mean in astrology?

In modern astrology, a symbol for change, independence, innovation, and departures from convention.

## How do you read it?

Read the planet's theme with its sign's expression style, the house's life area, and any calculated aspects; connect the symbols to the person's question and experience.

## Example

People born in the same period may share a Uranus sign; an individual reading needs additional chart evidence.

## A question to ask yourself

> In what specific situation would you like to try a different approach?

## What is the common misreading?

A Uranus sign alone cannot establish an individual’s rebellious traits or predict a sudden event.
